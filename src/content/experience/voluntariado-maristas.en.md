    The 10% Social Association (founded in 1973 in Ourense) is an initiative dedicated to supporting disadvantaged families. It has recently resumed its charitable work after the pandemic, focusing its current efforts on the monthly distribution of food. This project mobilizes a community of over 70 students and 20 teachers from the school each month, who are in charge of collecting, preparing, and delivering donations. The activity serves a dual purpose: providing vital end-of-month assistance to beneficiary families and offering students a valuable experience connecting with diverse social realities.
