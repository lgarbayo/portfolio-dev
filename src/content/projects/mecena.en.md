description: >-
  You create, Mecena handles the paperwork. Mobile-first web app built in a
  two-person team at 1hack 2026 for artists and small businesses in Spain: you
  talk about your work by voice and photograph it, and an AI agent builds your
  profile and dossier, finds the public and private calls you fit (real ones,
  from BDNS), drafts the application in your own words, scores it with a
  simulated jury against the official criteria and helps you justify the grant.
  Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Claude, with an MCP server and RevenueCat
  billing, deployed on Fly.io.
