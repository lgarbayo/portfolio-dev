title: "Mecena"
description: >-
  You create, Mecena handles the paperwork. Mobile-first web app built in a
  two-person team at 1hack 2026 for artists and small businesses in Spain: you
  choose whether you are a creator or a business, talk about your work by voice
  and photograph it, and Mecena builds your profile and dossier, finds the public
  and private calls you fit (real ones, from BDNS), drafts the application in
  your own words, scores it with a simulated jury against the official criteria
  and helps you justify the grant. It can run with no AI on the server: your own
  assistant improves the draft and plays the jury, in any chat or connected over
  MCP. Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Claude, with RevenueCat billing,
  deployed on Fly.io.
