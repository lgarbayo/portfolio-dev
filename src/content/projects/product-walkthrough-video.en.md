description: >-
  A Claude Code skill that turns a product, its repository and its running app,
  into a 2 to 5 minute explainer video. Claude reads the code and writes a script
  where every number has a source, and nothing is recorded until you approve it.
  Then three deterministic scripts take over: Playwright records the real app one
  chapter at a time, the voice-over comes from ElevenLabs, a local Kokoro voice or
  your own recordings, and HyperFrames renders it in the product's own colours and
  fonts. Each render is measured, not judged: loudness, music ducking, WCAG
  contrast and a frame per scene against its layout, plus subtitles and YouTube
  chapters ready to publish.
